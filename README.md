# Blinkit_sales_analysis

An end-to-end data analysis project aimed at understanding the sales performance of grocery items across multiple outlets of BlinkIT. This project explores various product and outlet characteristics to uncover actionable insights using **Python (pandas, matplotlib)** and **Excel**.

## 🔍 Key Objectives

- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing  
- Explore and visualize item-level and outlet-level sales performance  
- Identify high and low performing product categories  
- Analyze outlet impact on overall revenue  
- Understand customer preferences based on item fat content, visibility, and ratings  

## 📊 Key Insights

1. **Top Performing Categories**: "Fruits and Vegetables" and "Snack Foods" recorded the highest total sales across all outlets.  
2. **Outlet Type Impact**: Supermarket Type1 contributes the most to total sales, indicating strong consumer preference or better marketing strategy.  
3. **Sales vs Visibility**: Higher visibility generally led to increased sales, but some items with very low visibility still performed well, showing brand loyalty or necessity-based buying.  
4. **Effect of Outlet Size**: Medium-sized outlets consistently generated higher sales than both small and large outlets.  
5. **Fat Content Preference**: "Low Fat" items were sold slightly more frequently than "Regular", indicating a minor trend toward health consciousness.  
6. **Ratings Correlation**: Items with a 5-star rating had significantly higher average sales than lower-rated items.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

- **Python**: Data analysis using pandas, matplotlib  
- **Jupyter Notebook**: Exploratory Data Analysis and visual storytelling  
- **Excel**: Preliminary data exploration and filtering  
- **Matplotlib**: Custom visualizations
